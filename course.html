Pricing Your Work With Confidence

Work out what each piece really costs you, set retail and wholesale prices you can explain without apologizing, and stop discounting by default.

  • Level: Beginner
  • 4 modules
  • 16 lessons
  • 2 h 40 min of video

JHTaught by June Hartwell

What you will learn

  • Calculate the true cost of a product, including your time and overheads
  • Set retail and wholesale prices that both leave room for profit
  • Choose an hourly rate you can live on, not one you feel you deserve
  • Explain your prices calmly when a customer pushes back
  • Plan a price increase and tell customers without drama
  • Spot the products that quietly lose you money

Is this course for you?

A good fit if

  • You sell handmade or small-batch work and guessed your first prices
  • You are busy, but your bank balance does not show it
  • You want a repeatable method, not a magic number

Skip it if

  • You need tax or legal advice (we are not accountants)
  • You resell mass-produced stock and compete on price alone

Course questions

When do I get access?

Self-paced courses and workshop recordings open straight after checkout. Live cohorts open a welcome module one week before the first call.

What if I miss a live call?

Every call is recorded and posted within a day, with a short written summary. You can send your question beforehand and the teacher will answer it on the recording.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of buying, as long as you have watched less than a third of the lessons. Just reply to your receipt email.
