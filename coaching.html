What to expect

From booking to action plan

  1. Send your homework

    A short form arrives with your confirmation: your shop link, current prices or photos, and the one question that matters most.

  2. Meet on video

    We share a screen and work through your actual numbers and pages. Sessions are recorded for you if you want them to be.

  3. Get your plan

    Within two working days you get a written action plan with no more than five next steps, in the order to do them.

Policies

Booking questions

Can I reschedule?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before your session, using the link in your confirmation email. Inside 24 hours we will still try to help.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel more than 48 hours ahead for a full refund. After that, we can move your session once instead.

Which session should I choose?

If you are unsure about your numbers, choose the Pricing Review. If your prices are fine but sales are slow, choose the Shop and Brand Audit.

Do you offer packages?

The Studio + Coaching membership includes one 45 minute session every month. See membership plans.
