Before you enroll

Questions makers ask us

I am brand new. Where should I start?

Take the free Start Here: Your First Price List course. It takes about half an hour and tells you whether you need the full pricing course next.

How long do I keep access to a course?

Self-paced courses you buy are yours for as long as the school exists, including updates. Live cohorts include the calls plus 12 months of replays.

Is this tax or legal advice?

No. We teach practical business habits. For tax and legal questions in your country, please talk to a qualified professional.

What if a course is not right for me?

Email us within 14 days of buying and you get a full refund, as long as you have watched less than a third of the lessons.

Are lessons captioned?

Yes. Every video has edited captions and a downloadable transcript, and worksheets come as printable PDFs and fillable spreadsheets.
