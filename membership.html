Policies, plainly

Membership questions

Can I cancel or pause?

Yes. Cancel from your account page in two clicks and keep access until the end of the period you paid for. You can also pause for up to three months a year.

Can I switch plans later?

Any time. Upgrades start straight away and we credit the unused part of your current plan. Downgrades start at your next billing date.

What happens to my progress if I leave?

We keep your progress and worksheets for 12 months, so you can pick up where you left off if you come back.

Do you offer reduced prices?

We keep a number of reduced-price Library seats each quarter for makers on a low income. Email hello@fernhill.example and tell us a little about your work. No proof needed.

Do I need a membership to buy one course?

No. Every course can be bought on its own from the catalogue. Membership simply works out cheaper if you take two or more.
